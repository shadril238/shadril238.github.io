Hello, I’m Shadril Hassan Shifat.

AI Researcher · Software Engineer

I am a software engineer and researcher passionate about building intelligent systems that connect rigorous research with scalable engineering.

I am a Master’s student in Information and Communication Engineering at Yeungnam University on a KOICA Scholarship, and a Research Assistant in the Broadband Wireless Communication (BWC) Lab under Prof. Kwonhue Choi, working on AI for communication technology. I am also a Research Associate at ELITE Research Lab, focusing on LLM reasoning and agentic AI for low-resource languages.

Previously, I was a Software Engineer at Brain Station 23 PLC. (2024–2026), where I built high-performance distributed fintech systems.

Across research and industry, I care about the same things: models that reason and explain their decisions, and systems that are reliable, observable, and reproducible.
